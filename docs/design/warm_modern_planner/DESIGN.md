## Brand & Style

This design system expresses intentional calm, warmth, and daily clarity. It blends Scandinavian minimalism with soft tactile warmth, intentionally avoiding the sterile, corporate feeling of traditional productivity software while maintaining rigorous structural discipline.

### Personality & Tone
- **Warm & Reassuring:** Uses subtle peach-tinted atmospheric backgrounds to soften screen glare, establishing a gentle ritual for starting and ending each day.
- **Focused & Uncluttered:** Content surfaces are crisp and deliberate. Negative space actively manages cognitive load, highlighting priorities effortlessly.
- **Tactile & Responsive:** Inputs, checkboxes, and task cards mirror paper stationery with digital precision, delivering crisp, satisfying micro-interactions upon completion.

### Aesthetic Influences
- **Material 3 Foundation:** Systemic color roles, container layers, and accessible touch targets provide structural predictability.
- **Boutique Stationery:** Smooth rounded corners, delicate warm borders, and energetic red-orange highlights echo high-end physical journals and planners.

## Colors

The palette balances soft, glowing warm neutrals with a vibrant red-orange accent that conveys energy and focus without inducing panic.

### Light Mode Roles
- **Canvas / Atmosphere (`#FFF7F2`):** A pale, peachy-tinted warm base that frames nested content.
- **Surface / Card (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container surfaces that lift content cleanly from the warm canvas.
- **Surface Variant (`#FDF0E9`):** Subdued tint for selected states, chips, and muted secondary panels.
- **Border / Outline (`#F0E2DA`):** Low-contrast warm contour lines that frame cards and divider lines without visual heaviness.
- **Primary Accent (`#F05023`):** High-energy red-orange used for primary actions, floating action buttons, active tab indicators, and checked states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E07A5F`):** Terracotta-tinted accent for categories, tags, and shared collaborator avatars.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D9822B`):** Warm amber used for scheduled times, pending alerts, and reminder flags.
- **Text Primary (`#1C1917`):** Deep warm obsidian, ensuring high legibility and contrast against white surfaces.
- **Text Secondary (`#78716C`):** Warm stone gray for metadata, timestamps, and secondary descriptions.

### Dark Mode Adaptations
- **Canvas / Atmosphere (`#18181B`):** Neutral zinc-charcoal background.
- **Surface / Card (`#242427`):** Elevated charcoal container with warmth retained in subtle highlights.
- **Surface Variant (`#2E2E33`):** Neutral interactive fill for inputs and hover states.
- **Border / Outline (`#3F3F46`):** Soft, low-luminance boundary contour.
- **Primary Accent (`#FF6B4A`):** Slightly elevated red-orange with increased luminance for comfortable contrast on dark surfaces.
- **Text Primary (`#F4F4F5`):** Soft off-white to eliminate glare.
- **Text Secondary (`#A1A1AA`):** Neutral gray for supporting metadata.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles, leveraging its friendly geometric terminals, broad x-height, and clean legibility at compact sizes.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for daily date headers (e.g., "Wednesday, Oct 24") and view titles. Negative letter-spacing keeps titles tight, grounded, and modern.
- **Titles & Task Headings:** `title-md` and `title-sm` handle task descriptions and card titles, maintaining balanced weights that differentiate them from secondary metadata.
- **Body:** Clear and readable for expanded task notes, sub-bullet descriptions, and calendar invites.
- **Labels:** Crisp uppercase or small-caps treatments for tags, collaborator statuses, time blocks, and category markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 8pt layout rhythm designed to support single-column mobile task streams and responsive multi-column dashboard views for desktop screens.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single column stream. Outer margin: `1rem` (`margin`). Gutter: `1rem` (`gutter`). Action triggers are anchored along the bottom navigation bar or floating within safe reach.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 2-column layout (Sidebar navigation or calendar overview on the left; active daily task feed on the right). Margin: `2rem` (`margin-md`). Gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter-md`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 3-column layout (Persistent contextual calendar / navigation, active plan agenda, side drawer for item details and subtasks). Margin: `3rem` (`margin-lg`). Gutter: `2rem` (`gutter-lg`). Max content width: `1280px` centered.

### Spacing Principles
- Compact list layouts use `space-sm` (8px) gaps between checklist rows to maintain high information density while preserving comfortable minimum 44px touch envelopes.
- Interior card padding standardizes at `space-md` (16px) for compact widgets, expanding to `space-lg` (24px) for prominent day modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines distinct surface color separation with ambient warm-tinted shadows, keeping interfaces tactile without heavy skeuomorphism.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Peach-tinted `#FFF7F2` (Light) or zinc `#18181B` (Dark). Completely flat foundation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on canvas, enclosed by a 1px border of `#F0E2DA`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(240, 80, 35, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`. In dark mode, borders shift to `#3F3F46` with shadow `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Dragged Items / Popovers):** Elevated slightly during reordering, hovering, or contextual menu opening. Ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(240, 80, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Floating Daily Action Button):** Prominent floating focus. Shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(240, 80, 35, 0.16), 0 6px 12px -3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a softened, approachable geometry (`roundedness: 2`), balancing structured organization with tactile warmth.

### Shape Scales
- **Default Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, form input borders, inline tags, small badges, and context menu items.
- **Containers & Cards (1rem / 16px):** Daily agenda cards, scheduling modules, modal bottom sheets, and timeline sections (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pills & Overlays (Full Rounded):** Circular action buttons, category pill selectors, interactive status indicators, and collaborator avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled red-orange (`#F05023`), white label text, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-md` based on density. Includes subtle hover scale and warm shadow elevation.
- **Secondary / Tonal:** Light peach surface (`#FDF0E9`), primary colored text (`#F05023`), no border.
- **Outlined:** 1px border (`#F0E2DA`), text primary (`#1C1917`), background transparent, shifting to `#FFFFFF` on hover.

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Unchecked:** 20x20px container, `rounded-md` (6px), 1.5px border (`#D6C7C0`), smooth hover fill with `#FDF0E9`.
- **Checked:** Animated fill with `#F05023`, crisp white checkmark vector, simultaneous subtle strikethrough and 50% opacity transition applied to corresponding task label.

### Task List Items
- **Structure:** Clean 48px baseline height for standard touch access. Left-aligned checkbox, flexible title text, right-aligned time-tag and collaborator avatars.
- **Interaction:** Hovering reveals drag handles and quick-action menu triggers (reschedule, flag, delete). Background shifts from pure white to `#FDFBF9` upon hover.

### Input Fields
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#F0E2DA`, 12px vertical padding, 16px horizontal padding, `rounded-md`.
- **Focus State:** 1.5px border `#F05023` with a soft glow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(240, 80, 35, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default:** Height 28px, pill-shaped, background `#FDF0E9`, text color `#78716C`, 1px border in `#F0E2DA`.
- **Active / Selected:** Background `#F05023`, text color `#FFFFFF`, border disappears.

### Cards & Daily Modules
- Pure white background, `rounded-lg` (16px), 1px border `#F0E2DA`, Level 1 ambient elevation. Card headers feature clear date headings paired with quick progress summaries (e.g., "4/6 completed").

### Shared Daily Planner Specifics
- **Collaborator Facepile:** 24px overlapping circular avatars with a 2px white ring mask to distinguish simultaneous multi-user planning.
- **Time Block Strips:** Vertical left accent bars (3px wide, rounded ends) colored by category (e.g., red-orange for deep work, terracotta for meetings, amber for habits).
